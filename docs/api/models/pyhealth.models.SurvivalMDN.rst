pyhealth.models.SurvivalMDN
===================================

Survival Mixture Density Network for right-censored time-to-event prediction
(Han, Goldstein and Ranganath, MLHC 2022). The model predicts a full survival
curve per patient from a Gaussian mixture mapped to positive times, and trains
on the exact censored likelihood.
